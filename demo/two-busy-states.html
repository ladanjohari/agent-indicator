Why two busy states

Two sessions. The first writes, runs a command, writes again, finishes. The second starts a command that never exits.

Watch the second one. It stays alive. It never turns amber, because amber means it needs you and it does not. The only thing telling you it has gone wrong is that Running a command has been on screen far too long.

  • Idle write-docs 0s
  • Idle dev-server 0s

Merge the two into one busy state and the stuck session becomes indistinguishable from a healthy one. That is the whole argument.
